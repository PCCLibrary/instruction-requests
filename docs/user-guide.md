# Library Instruction Request System - User Guide

## 1. Introduction

### What is the Library Instruction Request System?

The Library Instruction Request System is a web application that streamlines the process of scheduling and managing library instruction sessions at Portland Community College. It connects faculty members who need library instruction with librarians who provide these services.

### Who Uses This System?

**Faculty Members**: Use a simple web form to submit requests for library instruction sessions. No login is required to submit requests.

**Librarians**: Access a dashboard to view, manage, assign, and track instruction requests through their complete lifecycle. Librarians log in using their PCC credentials.

### What This Guide Covers

This guide is divided into sections for different users:

- **Faculty**: How to submit instruction requests and what to expect
- **Librarians**: How to use the dashboard, manage requests, and work with all system features
- **Everyone**: Understanding notifications, common workflows, and troubleshooting

## 2. For Faculty: Submitting Instruction Requests

### 2.1 Accessing the Request Form

The instruction request form is available at [URL will be provided]. You do not need to log in or create an account to submit a request.

[Screenshot of instruction request form landing page]

### 2.2 Completing the Request Form

The form is divided into several sections. All fields marked with an asterisk (*) are required.

#### Instructor Information

**Your Name*** (First and Last)
Enter your full name as it should appear in correspondence.

**Your Email Address***
Provide your PCC email address. This is where you'll receive updates about your request.

**Phone Number** (Optional)
Include a phone number if you prefer to be contacted by phone.

[Screenshot of Instructor Information section]

**Note**: If you've submitted a request before using the same email address, your name will be automatically filled in when you start typing your email.

#### Course Information

**Campus***
Select the campus where the instruction session will take place:
- Cascade
- Rock Creek
- Southeast
- Sylvania
- Online (for fully online courses)

[Screenshot of Campus dropdown]

**Course Subject & Number***
Enter the course subject (e.g., "WR" for Writing) and course number (e.g., "121").

**CRN** (Optional)
The Course Reference Number from your course schedule.

**Course Title***
The full title of your course (e.g., "Academic Literacy and Critical Inquiry").

**Number of Credits***
The credit hours for the course.

**Number of Students***
Estimated enrollment for the course.

**Section Type***
Select whether your course is:
- Online
- Hybrid
- In-Person

[Screenshot of Course Information section]

#### Instruction Session Details

**Instruction Location***
Where will the instruction take place? Options include:
- Specific library location at your campus
- Your classroom
- Online (via Zoom or other platform)

**Preferred Date/Time***
Provide your preferred date(s) and time(s) for the instruction session. Be as specific as possible, and include alternative options if available.

Example: "Tuesday, October 15 at 10:00 AM, or Thursday, October 17 at 2:00 PM"

**Duration***
How long should the session be? (e.g., "50 minutes", "1 hour", "75 minutes")

[Screenshot of Instruction Session Details section]

#### Instruction Needs

**Number of Sessions Needed***
Indicate how many instruction sessions you need. Most courses require one session, but you can request multiple sessions if needed.

**Research Assignment Description***
Describe the research assignment or project your students will be working on. This helps the librarian tailor the instruction to your course needs.

Example: "Students will write a 5-page research paper on a social issue of their choice using at least 3 scholarly sources."

**Specific Instruction Requests**
Let us know if there are specific topics, databases, or skills you'd like covered in the session.

Example: "Please cover how to find peer-reviewed articles and how to evaluate sources for credibility."

**Subject Areas**
List the subject areas or disciplines relevant to your course research assignment.

Example: "Sociology, Criminal Justice, Psychology"

[Screenshot of Instruction Needs section]

#### File Uploads (Optional)

You can attach files to help the librarian understand your instruction needs. Common files include:
- Course syllabus
- Research assignment description
- Assignment rubric
- Sample topics or guidelines

**File Requirements**:
- Maximum file size: 20 MB per file
- Maximum 4 files total
- Accepted formats: PDF, Word (.doc, .docx), PowerPoint (.ppt, .pptx), text (.txt), and RTF

**How to Upload Files**:
1. Click the "Choose Files" button or drag files into the upload area
2. Select one or more files from your computer
3. Files will appear in the upload area once selected
4. To remove a file, click the X icon next to the filename

[Screenshot of file upload section with files uploaded]

### 2.3 Submitting Your Request

Once you've completed all required fields:

1. Review your information for accuracy
2. Click the "Submit Request" button at the bottom of the form
3. You'll see a confirmation message on screen
4. You'll receive a confirmation email at the address you provided

[Screenshot of submit button and confirmation message]

### 2.4 After Submission

#### What Happens Next

1. **Immediate Confirmation**: You'll receive an automated email confirming we received your request
2. **Assignment**: A scheduling librarian will assign your request to a teaching librarian based on subject expertise and availability
3. **Acceptance**: The assigned librarian will review and accept your request
4. **Scheduling**: The librarian will contact you to finalize details and schedule the session
5. **Session**: The instruction session takes place

#### Email Notifications You'll Receive

You'll automatically receive email notifications at these stages:

**Request Received** (Immediate)
Confirms we've received your request and provides your request details.

**Librarian Assigned**
Informs you which librarian has been assigned to your request and includes their contact information.

**Request Accepted**
Confirms that the librarian has reviewed and accepted your request. They may reach out to you directly to coordinate scheduling details.

[Screenshot of sample notification email]

### 2.5 Request Status

You can check the status of your request by contacting the library or the assigned librarian directly. Request statuses include:

- **Received**: Your request has been submitted and is awaiting assignment
- **Assigned**: A librarian has been assigned to your request
- **Accepted**: The librarian has reviewed and accepted your request
- **Scheduled**: Your session has been added to the librarian's calendar
- **Completed**: Your instruction session has taken place

### 2.6 Making Changes to Your Request

If you need to update information after submitting your request:

1. Contact the library directly at [contact information]
2. If a librarian has been assigned, you can contact them directly using the information in your notification email
3. Provide your request details and the changes you need to make

## 3. For Librarians: Using the Dashboard

### 3.1 Accessing the System

To access the Library Instruction Request System dashboard:

1. Go to [URL will be provided]
2. Click "Login" or you'll be automatically redirected to the PCC Single Sign-On page
3. Log in using your PCC credentials
4. You'll be redirected to the dashboard

[Screenshot of dashboard home page]

### 3.2 Understanding the Dashboard

The dashboard displays instruction requests in a table format with multiple tabs for different views.

#### Dashboard Tabs

**My Requests**
Shows only requests that have been assigned to you. This is where you'll find requests you need to review, accept, or manage.

**Campus Requests**
Displays all requests for the campus(es) you're associated with. Scheduling librarians use this view to assign requests to teaching librarians.

**All Requests** (if available)
Shows all requests across all campuses. Availability depends on your role and permissions.

[Screenshot of dashboard tabs]

#### Table Columns

The request table includes the following information:

- **ID**: Unique identifier for the request
- **Instructor**: Name of the faculty member who submitted the request
- **Course**: Course subject, number, and title
- **Campus**: Where the instruction will take place
- **Status**: Current status of the request (Received, Assigned, Accepted, Scheduled, Completed)
- **Assigned To**: Name of the teaching librarian (if assigned)
- **Created**: Date the request was submitted
- **Actions**: Quick action buttons for viewing or managing the request

[Screenshot of request table with columns highlighted]

#### Sorting and Filtering

You can organize the table to find requests more easily:

- **Sort**: Click on column headers to sort by that field (ascending/descending)
- **Filter**: Use the search box to filter requests by instructor name, course, or other criteria
- **Per Page**: Adjust how many requests display per page

[Screenshot of sorting and filtering options]

### 3.3 Request Workflow & Statuses

#### Status Definitions

**Received**
A new request that has been submitted by faculty but not yet assigned to a teaching librarian. Scheduling librarians should review and assign these requests.

**Assigned**
A request that has been assigned to a specific teaching librarian but not yet accepted. If you see a request with this status in your "My Requests" tab, you need to review and accept it.

**Accepted**
The assigned librarian has reviewed and accepted the request. Next steps include coordinating with the instructor and scheduling the session.

**Scheduled**
The instruction session has been added to Google Calendar. The session is confirmed and on the calendar.

**Completed**
The instruction session has taken place. The request is closed.

**Rejected**
The request was declined by an assigned librarian and needs to be reassigned to another librarian.

[Screenshot of status badges/indicators]

#### How Requests Move Through Statuses

1. Faculty submits request → **Received**
2. Scheduling librarian assigns to teaching librarian → **Assigned**
3. Teaching librarian accepts → **Accepted**
4. Librarian creates Google Calendar event → **Scheduled**
5. Session takes place, librarian marks complete → **Completed**

Alternative path: If a teaching librarian rejects a request, it returns to **Rejected** status and can be reassigned.

### 3.4 Working with Requests

#### 3.4.1 Viewing Request Details

To view the full details of a request:

1. Click on the request row in the table, or
2. Click the "View" or "Edit" action button

This opens the request detail page showing:
- Instructor contact information
- Complete course details
- Instruction session requirements
- Research assignment description
- Uploaded files (if any)
- Request history and status changes
- Comments (if any)

[Screenshot of request detail page]

#### 3.4.2 Assigning Requests (For Scheduling Librarians)

Scheduling librarians are responsible for assigning new requests to teaching librarians.

**To assign a request:**

1. Navigate to the "Campus Requests" tab
2. Find a request with "Received" status
3. Click to open the request details
4. Look for the "Assign To" dropdown or section
5. Select the appropriate teaching librarian from the list
6. Click "Save" or "Assign"

[Screenshot of assign librarian dropdown]

**Choosing the Right Librarian:**
- Consider subject expertise
- Check availability
- Consider workload balance
- Review any special requests from the instructor

Once assigned, the teaching librarian will receive an email notification and the request status changes to "Assigned."

#### 3.4.3 Accepting Requests (For Teaching Librarians)

When a request is assigned to you, you'll receive an email notification.

**To accept a request:**

1. Go to the "My Requests" tab
2. Find requests with "Assigned" status
3. Click to open the request details
4. Review all information:
   - Instructor contact details
   - Course information
   - Instruction needs
   - Preferred dates/times
   - Any uploaded files
5. Click the "Accept" button

[Screenshot of accept button on request detail page]

After accepting:
- The status changes to "Accepted"
- The instructor receives an email notification
- You can begin coordinating scheduling details with the instructor

**If You Need to Reject a Request:**

If you're unable to handle a request (scheduling conflict, subject outside expertise, etc.):

1. Open the request details
2. Click the "Reject" button
3. (Optional) Add a comment explaining why you're rejecting it
4. Confirm the rejection

The request status changes to "Rejected" and scheduling librarians are notified to reassign it.

[Screenshot of reject button and confirmation]

#### 3.4.4 Edit Locking

The system uses edit locking to prevent multiple librarians from editing the same request simultaneously.

**What You'll See:**

When you open a request for editing, the system automatically locks it. Other users will see a message indicating:
- The request is currently being edited
- Who is editing it
- When they started editing

[Screenshot of edit lock message]

**Inactivity Warning:**

If you're editing a request but haven't made changes for about 14 minutes, you'll see a warning message. This reminds you to save your work or the lock will be released.

[Screenshot of inactivity warning]

**Lock Release:**

Locks are automatically released when:
- You save your changes and close the request
- You navigate away from the request
- You've been inactive for 15 minutes

**What if Someone Else is Editing:**

If you try to open a request that's locked by another user:
- You'll see who is editing it
- You can view the request in read-only mode
- Wait for them to finish, or
- Contact them directly if it's urgent

#### 3.4.5 Managing Request Details

You can edit various fields in a request to update information or add details.

**To edit a request:**

1. Open the request detail page
2. Click the "Edit" button or pencil icon next to the field you want to change
3. Make your changes
4. Click "Save" to apply the changes

[Screenshot of edit mode for request fields]

**Commonly Edited Fields:**
- Instruction date/time (once coordinated with instructor)
- Session duration
- Location details
- Additional notes or requirements

**Important**: Always save your changes before navigating away or the lock will be released without saving.

### 3.6 Google Calendar Integration

The system integrates with Google Calendar to help you schedule and track instruction sessions.

#### 3.6.1 Creating Calendar Events

Once you've accepted a request and coordinated the final date/time with the instructor, you can create a Google Calendar event.

**Prerequisites:**
- Request must be in "Accepted" status
- Date/time and duration must be entered and saved in the request

**To create a calendar event:**

1. Open the request detail page
2. Ensure the date/time and duration fields are filled in
3. Look for the "Create Google Calendar Event" button
4. Click the button
5. A modal or form will appear with pre-filled information:
   - Event title (includes course and instructor name)
   - Date and time
   - Duration
   - Location
   - Attendees (instructor email)
6. Review the information
7. Make any necessary adjustments
8. Click "Create Event"

[Screenshot of Create Google Calendar Event button and modal]

**What Happens:**
- The event is created in the appropriate campus Google Calendar
- The instructor is added as an attendee and receives a calendar invitation
- The request status automatically changes to "Scheduled"
- A link to the calendar event appears in the request details

[Screenshot of scheduled request with calendar event link]

#### 3.6.2 Managing Calendar Events

**Viewing the Calendar Event:**
- Click the calendar event link in the request details
- This opens the event in Google Calendar

**Deleting a Calendar Event:**

If you need to cancel or reschedule:

1. Open the request detail page
2. Find the calendar event section
3. Click "Delete Calendar Event" button
4. Confirm the deletion
5. The event is removed from Google Calendar
6. The request status changes back to "Accepted"

[Screenshot of Delete Calendar Event button]

**Note**: If you need to reschedule, delete the existing event and create a new one with the updated date/time.

## 4. Notifications

### 4.1 Email Notifications Overview

The system automatically sends email notifications to keep everyone informed about request status changes. Notifications include relevant information and direct links to the request.

### 4.2 Notification Types

#### For Faculty

**Request Received**
**When**: Immediately after submitting a request
**Contains**: Confirmation of submission, request details, and what to expect next

**Librarian Assigned**
**When**: A librarian has been assigned to your request
**Contains**: Name and contact information of the assigned librarian, request details

**Request Accepted**
**When**: The assigned librarian has accepted your request
**Contains**: Confirmation that the librarian will handle your session, next steps

#### For Librarians

**New Request Notification** (Scheduling Librarians)
**When**: A new request is submitted for your campus
**Contains**: Instructor information, course details, link to assign the request

**When**: A request has been assigned to you
**Contains**: Complete request details, instructor contact, link to accept/reject

**Rejection Notification** (Scheduling Librarians)
**When**: A teaching librarian rejects an assigned request
**Contains**: Information about the rejected request, link to reassign

## 5. Common Workflows

### 5.1 Faculty Workflow

**Submitting and Tracking a Request:**

1. Access the instruction request form
2. Complete all required fields and upload any relevant files
3. Submit the request
4. Receive email confirmation (Request Received)
5. Receive notification when a librarian is assigned
6. Receive notification when the librarian accepts
7. Librarian contacts you to finalize scheduling
8. Attend the instruction session

### 5.2 Scheduling Librarian Workflow

**Managing New Requests:**

1. Receive email notification of new request
2. Log in to dashboard and navigate to Campus Requests
3. Review the request details:
   - Instructor information
   - Course requirements
   - Subject areas
   - Preferred dates/times
4. Select appropriate teaching librarian based on:
   - Subject expertise
   - Availability
   - Workload
5. Assign the request
6. Teaching librarian receives notification

**Handling Rejections:**

1. Receive notification of rejected request
2. Review rejection reason (if provided in comments)
3. Reassign to another teaching librarian
4. New librarian receives assignment notification

### 5.3 Teaching Librarian Workflow

**From Assignment to Completion:**

1. Receive email notification of assignment
2. Log in to dashboard and go to My Requests
3. Review request details thoroughly
4. Accept the request (or reject if unable to handle)
5. Contact the instructor to coordinate:
   - Final date and time
   - Location confirmation
   - Any additional requirements
6. Update request with finalized details
7. Create Google Calendar event
8. Upload any teaching materials or handouts
9. Conduct the instruction session
10. Mark the request as complete

## 6. Troubleshooting

### 6.1 For Faculty

**Form Won't Submit**
- Ensure all required fields (marked with *) are completed
- Check that your email address is valid
- Verify file uploads are under 20 MB each
- Try refreshing the page and resubmitting

**File Upload Issues**
- Confirm file is in an accepted format (PDF, Word, PowerPoint, text, RTF)
- Ensure file size is under 20 MB
- Try uploading files one at a time
- Check your internet connection

**Not Receiving Emails**
- Check your spam/junk folder
- Verify you entered your email address correctly
- Add the system email to your safe senders list
- Contact the library if emails continue to be missing

**How to Update a Submitted Request**
- Contact the library directly or the assigned librarian (if notified)
- Provide your request ID and the changes needed

### 6.2 For Librarians

**Can't Access the System**
- Verify you're using your correct PCC credentials
- Ensure you're on the PCC network or VPN
- Clear your browser cache and cookies
- Try a different browser
- Contact IT support if issues persist

**Can't Edit a Request (Locked)**
- Check who is currently editing (shown in the lock message)
- Wait for them to finish editing
- Contact them directly if urgent
- If the lock is stale (user left without releasing), it will automatically release after 15 minutes

**File Upload Issues**
- Confirm file meets requirements (size, format)
- Ensure you have a stable internet connection
- Try uploading one file at a time
- Refresh the page and try again

**Calendar Event Creation Fails**
- Verify date/time and duration are filled in and saved
- Ensure request status is "Accepted"
- Check your Google Calendar permissions
- Try refreshing the page
- Contact support if issue persists

**Not Receiving Notifications**
- Check your spam/junk folder
- Verify your email address is correct in your profile
- Ensure notifications are enabled in system settings
- Contact support to verify notification system status

## 7. Best Practices

### 7.1 For Faculty

**When to Submit Requests**
- Submit requests at least 2-3 weeks before your preferred instruction date
- Earlier submissions allow more flexibility for scheduling
- Consider librarian availability during peak times (mid-term, finals)

**How Much Detail to Provide**
- Be specific about your research assignment requirements
- Include learning objectives if possible
- Mention any databases or resources you'd like covered
- Provide alternative date/time options

**What Files to Attach**
- Course syllabus helps librarians understand context
- Assignment description ensures tailored instruction
- Rubrics help librarians focus on assessment criteria
- Sample topics give librarians a sense of scope

### 7.2 For Librarians

**Responding to Requests Promptly**
- Check for new assignments daily
- Accept or reject requests within 1-2 business days
- Contact instructors quickly after accepting

**Using Comments Effectively**
- Document important decisions or changes
- Communicate scheduling conflicts or issues
- Leave notes for other librarians who may need context
- Record why a request was rejected (helps with future assignments)

**Managing Your Assigned Requests**
- Prioritize requests by submission date and preferred dates
- Keep request details updated as you coordinate with instructors
- Use the calendar integration to stay organized
- Mark requests complete promptly after sessions

**Keeping Calendar Up to Date**
- Create calendar events as soon as dates are finalized
- Delete or update events if plans change
- Include all relevant details in calendar invitations
